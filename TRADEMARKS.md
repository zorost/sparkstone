# Sparkstone trademark policy

The code is open (Apache-2.0); the name and logos are not. This keeps "Sparkstone" meaning the real thing: builds
that people can trust, and a reputation that stays with the project.

"Sparkstone", "Sparkstone Twin" and the logos in `assets/brand/` are trademarks of Zoros. The Apache-2.0 license
does not cover them (section 6).

## You may, without asking

- Say your work is **"built with Sparkstone"**, **"based on Sparkstone"** or **"compatible with Sparkstone"**.
- Use the name and unmodified logo in articles, talks, videos, benchmarks and reviews about Sparkstone.
- Distribute **unmodified** official releases under the Sparkstone name.
- Use the logo to link to the official repository or website.

## Ask first (hello@zoros.com)

- Use the name or logo in a product name, company name, domain or social handle.
- Ship a **modified** build under the Sparkstone name. A fork must use a different name and may say
  "a fork of Sparkstone".
- Use the logo on merchandise, or in a way that suggests Zoros sponsors or endorses you.
- Offer paid services (hosting, support, consulting) marketed under the Sparkstone name.

## Never

- Change the logo's shape or colours, or combine it with other marks.
- Use the name in a way that confuses people about who made a build or who supports it.

Third-party names (NVIDIA, DGX Spark, Basalt, Inkling) belong to their owners. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).
